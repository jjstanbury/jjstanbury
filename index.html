James Stanbury Music

Hi!

​

I'm a Detroit-based multi-instrumentalist/music educator with 20+ years of experience, performing jazz, rock, funk, and more. I've toured nationally/internationally and perform in projects ranging from solo guitar to orchestras. My experience includes weddings, corporate events, musicals, bar/venues, as well as musical instruction.

About

Services

Guitar

I offer solo guitar performances for wedding ceremonies, instrumental music for cocktail hours, and live entertainment for bars, clubs, and private events. I also offer session work, having contributed guitar parts and solos to numerous records. 

​

Some businesses I've worked with/at are The Detroit Athletic Club, Detroit's Orchestra Hall, Cliff Bells, and many more!

Bass

Similar to guitar I offer sub work, session recordings, and musicals. I'm a proficient reader having studied Bass at Wayne State University and participated in various Jazz ensembles and small groups. 

​​​

Other

I try to do a little bit of everything and have experience in many fields in the musical world.

​

Instruments I've played on records include Cello, Pedal Steel, and Banjo. I've even done an entire wedding with just a Ukulele. Let me know what you need and I'll make it happen!

Musicals are some of my favorite gigs. Some musicals I've performed are Avenue Q, Annie, Into the Woods, Beauty and the Beast, Grease, and Mama Mia, to name a few. 

​​

I also have an extensive background in recording studios from tuning the acoustic piano to running full sessions. I'm currently an Audio Technician for a leading competitive brand in the Studio Recording Business. 

​

Lessons

​

I've taught both guitar and bass for 15+ years. I've even taught some piano and ukulele lessons as well. 

​

A good musician is always learning and I still take lessons to this day from various teachers. I've attended  masterclasses with Pat Metheny, Benny Golson, Julian Lage, Brian Blade, Gilad Hekselman, and many more.
